The rulebook

How a SPOILS hunt works.

A live trail, clear rules and a real reward for the hunter(s) who solve it first. The mystery is in the story. The rules are all here.

Join the hunt

Before the hunt.

  1. Create your account.

    Sign up or log in to your hunter account, and make sure all the information you provide matches your valid ID. We use it to verify winners and reach you about your reward.

  2. Choose your hunt.

    Each hunt page shows the start time, the reward and the entry fee. Some hunts are free and most are paid.

  3. Be ready.

    Join the Hunters Club on WhatsApp for hunt alerts and a reminder before the first clue arrives.

During the hunt.

The hunt is live.

The first clue arrives at start time, for everyone at the same moment.

Every answer opens the next stage.

Clues lead to real places online: a web page, a social post, a detail hidden in an app. Find the answer, enter it on SPOILS, and the next clue opens.

Hunts move at different speeds.

A short hunt can be over in minutes. A longer one with more stages can take hours. It depends on the trail.

Stuck? Look again.

Some hunts have hints, others don't, and in some hunts you buy hints when you need them. Often the answer is hiding in something you've already found.

Hunt solo

Sharing answers gets you removed.

Every hunter plays on their own account. Race your friends, but each of you solves alone.

After the hunt.

  1. The hunt closes.

    The hunters who finish within the hunt's winning places claim the SPOILS. Every hunt states its number of winners in advance, with the reward for each place, from first to last. We check the results and contact every winner directly.

  2. The reward arrives.

    After a quick verification, cash goes to your bank account or SPOILS wallet. Non-cash rewards like event tickets and food vouchers reach you within 5 to 7 working days.

  3. Your privacy stays yours.

    We keep winners' names off our website. If you'd like to share your story, we'll make content with you, but only if you want to.

The rules in brief.

  • AgeYou must be 18 or older to play.
  • LocationSPOILS is open to hunters in Nigeria.
  • One account per personExtra accounts are removed and can't win.
  • Solo playFriends can compete against each other, but no teams and no shared answers.
  • How winners are decidedCorrect final answers and finish times are recorded by the platform. Every hunt states its number of winners in advance, with the reward for each place, from first to last.
  • VerificationWinners confirm their identity and phone number before receiving a reward.
  • Rewards as listedThe reward on the hunt page, cash or otherwise, is the reward you get.
  • Team membersSPOILS staff and anyone who helped build a hunt can't compete for its reward.
