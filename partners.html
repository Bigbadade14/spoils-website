For brands and events

Turn your brand into something people want to discover.

Most marketing asks people to watch. A SPOILS hunt gives them a reason to look for you. We design competitive treasure hunts where your products, posts and spaces become part of the trail.

Plan a hunt with us

What your brand can become in a hunt.

  • Your product becomes the evidence, or the key to the next stage.
  • Your social posts carry the clue, the contradiction or the hidden signal.
  • Your website becomes part of the search.
  • Your event becomes a live stage of the hunt.
  • Your brand history becomes the story hunters investigate.
  • Your packaging holds the detail that unlocks progress.

How a partner hunt works.

  1. We learn your story.

    A launch, a campaign, an event. We find the details worth turning into clues.

  2. We build the trail.

    We design a hunt where hunters have to look closely at your brand to move forward.

  3. Hunters compete.

    They join the hunt, race through the stages and talk about it while they play.

  4. Hunters claim the SPOILS.

    You choose the reward, cash or otherwise. We verify the winners, deliver the rewards and send you a summary of how the hunt went.

What to expect.

Partner hunts are new, so we run them as pilots. Before the hunt, we agree what success looks like for you, such as how many people take part, how far they get and how much time they spend with your brand. After the hunt, you get a report on what happened.

Good fits.

  • Tech and startup eventsthat want a competition running alongside the programme
  • Brands launchinga product or website they want people to explore properly
  • Universitiesand student communities
  • Media and creatorswho want a game for their audience
