What is SPOILS?

SPOILS is a competitive entertainment platform based in Nigeria. We run digital treasure hunts where participants, called hunters (yes, that's you), find the answers to clues hidden across real websites, social media pages and the internet, solve each stage, and race other hunters to the finish.

The fastest hunters to solve every stage claim the SPOILS: a real reward such as cash, event tickets, food vouchers or gadgets. Prizes can be anything.

Three steps

How SPOILS works.

  1. Join the hunt.

    Sign up or log in to your SPOILS account.

  2. Find what others miss.

    Every answer leads to the next clue. Look closer, connect the details and keep moving, because other hunters are searching too.

  3. Claim the SPOILS.

    Solve the final stage within the winning places, and the reward is yours. Every hunt states how many hunters can win before you join.

Quick questions

Is SPOILS gambling?
No. Results depend on what hunters notice, solve and how quickly they do it. Nobody wins by chance.
Who can play?
Anyone in Nigeria aged 18 or over.
Is it free?
Some hunts are free and most have an entry fee. The fee is always on the hunt page before you join.
How do winners receive their reward?
After a quick verification, cash goes to your bank account or SPOILS wallet. Other rewards reach you within 5 to 7 working days.
