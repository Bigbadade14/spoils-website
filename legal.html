Terms of Use

Version 2.0 · Published 24 October 2026 · In effect from 7 November 2026. Replaces Version 1.0 (April 2026).

The short version. You must be 18 or older and in Nigeria to play. One person, one account. Every hunt is solo, and it's won by skill and speed, never by chance. Each hunt shows its entry fee, number of winners and rewards before you join. Winners are verified before any reward is paid. Cheating, sharing answers or using extra accounts gets you removed. This summary helps you read the Terms. It doesn't replace them.

01Who we are and what this covers

SPOILS is operated by SPOILS Technology Limited, a company registered in Nigeria (RC 9438395), with its office in Lagos ("SPOILS", "we", "us"). These Terms of Use ("Terms") are an agreement between you and us. They cover your use of spoils.live, app.spoils.live, our community channels and every hunt we run (together, the "Platform").

By creating an account, joining a hunt or using the Platform, you agree to these Terms, to the rules published on each hunt, and to our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle your data.

02Words we use

  • Hunter: anyone with a SPOILS account who takes part in a hunt.
  • Hunt: a timed competition in which hunters solve a chain of clues. Clues may point to web pages, social media posts, apps or other places online, and occasionally to physical locations.
  • Stage: one step in a hunt. You open the next stage by entering the correct answer or stage code.
  • Hunt page: the page in the app that shows a hunt's start time, entry fee, number of winners, rewards for each place and any rules specific to that hunt ("Hunt Rules").
  • Reward: anything a winner receives, such as cash, wallet credit, vouchers, tickets, goods or experiences.
  • Vault: your SPOILS wallet, where you can add funds, pay entry fees or buy hints, and receive cash rewards.

03Who can play

To create an account and take part in hunts, you must:

  • be at least 18 years old and able to enter into a binding contract under Nigerian law;
  • live in Nigeria and take part from Nigeria. SPOILS is open only to hunters in Nigeria for now;
  • give true, current and complete details that match a valid government-issued ID;
  • be free of any SPOILS suspension or ban.

SPOILS staff, contractors and anyone who helped create, test or run a hunt, or who saw its answers in advance, may not compete for that hunt's rewards. The same applies to their immediate family and household members.

04Your account

  • One account per person. Extra or duplicate accounts will be closed, and any account linked to them cannot win.
  • Keep it secure. You are responsible for activity on your account. Keep your password private. If you think someone else has accessed your account, tell us at hello@spoils.live straight away.
  • Sign-in options. You can sign up with email and password or with Google. We may ask you to confirm your email address or phone number.
  • Your hunter name. Leaderboards, activity feeds and public profiles show your hunter name, never your legal name. Choose one that is not offensive, misleading or someone else's identity or brand.

05How hunts work

Skill, never chance. SPOILS hunts are competitions of observation, reasoning, research and speed. Winners are decided by correct answers and the finish times recorded by the Platform. Nothing in a SPOILS hunt is decided by chance, random draw or by predicting the outcome of an event.

  • Before you join, the hunt page tells you the start time, the entry fee (or that it's free), how many winners there will be, and the reward for each winning place.
  • At start time, every hunter receives the first clue at the same moment. Each correct answer opens the next stage.
  • Hints. Some hunts have free hints, some have none, and in some you can buy hints. A hunt page will say which applies.
  • Places and limits. Some hunts have a cap on the number of hunters. When a hunt is full, you may be placed on a reserve list. If a place opens, you'll be offered it in reserve-list order.
  • Hunt Rules. Each hunt may have its own rules. If Hunt Rules conflict with these Terms, these Terms apply, unless the Hunt Rules expressly say they replace a specific part of them for that hunt.

06How winners are decided

  • Winning places go to the hunters whose correct final answers are recorded first by our servers, in order, up to the number of winners stated on the hunt page. Our server's record of the time is final for ranking purposes.
  • If two or more hunters are recorded at exactly the same time for a winning place, the rewards for the tied places are added together and shared equally between them, unless the Hunt Rules say otherwise.
  • Leaderboards and results shown during a hunt are provisional. Results become final after our review, which normally takes place within 48 hours of the hunt closing.
  • If you think a result is wrong, email hello@spoils.live within 48 hours of the results being published. We will review it and reply with our decision and the reasons for it.

07Entry fees, the Vault and refunds

  • Payments. Entry fees, hint purchases and Vault top-ups are paid in naira through our licensed payment partners, currently Paystack and Flutterwave. We never see or store your full card details.
  • The Vault. Your Vault balance can only be used on SPOILS. It is not a bank account or savings product, and it doesn't earn interest. You can withdraw cash rewards and unused funds to a Nigerian bank account in your own name, after verification. Payment-provider charges, where they apply, are shown before you confirm.
  • When you get a refund. If we cancel a hunt before its first clue is released, or void a hunt because of a fault on our side, your entry fee is refunded in full to your Vault or to your original payment method.
  • When there's no refund. Entry fees are not refundable if you miss the start, leave before finishing, don't finish within the winning places, or are disqualified for breaking these Terms. A hint you have bought and opened is not refundable.
  • Your rights under the law. Nothing in these Terms limits any right you have under the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018 or any other law that protects consumers.

08Rewards

  • Rewards as listed. The reward shown on the hunt page for your winning place is the reward you get.
  • Verification first. Before any reward is released, winners confirm their identity and phone number, and we check that the win was fair. The name on your account, your ID and any bank account you use must match.
  • Claim window. We contact every winner directly. If a winner doesn't complete verification within 7 days of our first message, or the Hunt Rules' own deadline, the reward may pass to the next eligible hunter or be held back.
  • Delivery. Cash rewards are paid to your Vault or by bank transfer to a Nigerian bank account in your name, normally within 3 working days after verification. Non-cash rewards such as tickets, vouchers and goods normally reach you within 5 to 7 working days after verification.
  • Partner rewards. Some rewards are provided by partner brands. If a listed reward becomes unavailable for reasons outside our control, we will give you a replacement of equal or greater value, or its cash value where you prefer that.
  • Rewards are personal. A reward can't be transferred to someone else or exchanged, unless we agree.
  • Taxes. You are responsible for any tax that applies to rewards you receive. Where the law requires us to deduct tax before paying a reward, we will do so and tell you.
  • Your privacy as a winner. We keep winners' legal names off our website. With your permission, we may share your story, hunter name or photo. You can withdraw that permission at any time.

09Fair play

Breaking any of these rules can lead to disqualification, loss of rewards, suspension or a permanent ban. In serious cases, we may report the matter to the appropriate authorities.

  • Hunt solo. Every hunt is played alone. Sharing answers, stage codes or hints with anyone during a live hunt, or working as a team, is not allowed.
  • No extra help from machines. No bots, scripts, automated tools or attempts to scrape, reverse-engineer, overload or tamper with the Platform. Searching the web, reading and thinking are allowed. Using AI tools is allowed unless the Hunt Rules say otherwise.
  • No exploiting faults. If you find a bug or a way to see answers early, report it to us. Using it is cheating.
  • No fake identities. No false details, borrowed IDs or bank accounts, and no impersonating anyone.
  • Spoilers wait. Don't post answers, stage codes or solutions anywhere while a hunt is live.
  • Referrals. Referral rewards are for genuinely new hunters. Self-referrals, fake sign-ups and referral farming will be reversed.

10Hunting in the real world and online

Clues can lead you to public websites, social media accounts, apps and, occasionally, physical places. While hunting:

  • follow the law and the terms of every platform you visit;
  • never try to access any account, system or private area you aren't allowed into;
  • don't contact, message, spam or harass people, businesses or accounts that appear in a clue, unless the clue clearly invites you to;
  • for physical locations, stay safe, respect private property, follow local rules and never hunt while driving.

Report any safety concern to hello@spoils.live or call or WhatsApp +234 810 429 1619.

We may delay, pause, change, re-run, void or cancel a hunt if there's a technical fault, a safety concern, a breach of these Terms, a legal or regulatory requirement, or an event outside our control. If this affects a paid hunt, section 07 explains your refund. If a hunt is voided after it has started, we will either re-run it for the hunters who entered or refund their entry fees.

Our WhatsApp community, social media pages and any comment or chat feature are part of the Platform. Be respectful. Harassment, hate speech, threats, scams, spam and sharing other people's personal information are not allowed, and we may remove anyone who breaks these rules.

You can close your account at any time from your account settings, where available, or by emailing hello@spoils.live. Your remaining Vault balance will be paid out to you after verification.

We may suspend or close your account if you break these Terms, if we reasonably suspect fraud, or if the law requires us to. Where we can, we'll tell you why and give you a chance to respond. If we close your account for cheating or fraud, you lose any rewards connected to that conduct. We will still return Vault funds you deposited yourself, minus any amount linked to the cheating or fraud, unless the law requires us to hold them.

SPOILS runs skill-based competitions and keeps them in line with the laws that apply in Nigeria. If a law, regulator or licence condition requires it, we may change how a hunt works, limit paid hunts in particular states, or pause a feature. We will tell you if this affects a hunt you have joined.

These Terms are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. If you have a complaint, contact us first. Most issues are resolved quickly. This doesn't affect your right to complain to the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission or any other regulator.

Privacy Policy

Version 2.0 · Published 24 October 2026 · In effect from 7 November 2026. Replaces the Privacy Policy and the Data & Compliance Policy (Version 1.0, April 2026).

The short version. We collect what we need to run hunts, keep them fair, verify winners and pay rewards. We never sell your data. Leaderboards show your hunter name, never your legal name. We keep winners' names off our website. You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your data at any time. This policy follows the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the NDPA General Application and Implementation Directive 2025 (GAID).

01Who is responsible for your data

SPOILS Technology Limited (RC 9438395), Lagos, Nigeria, is the data controller for personal data processed through the Platform. For any privacy question or request, email hello@spoils.live with "Privacy request" in the subject line, or call +234 810 429 1619.

02What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number, hunter name, password (stored only as a secure hash) and, if you use Google sign-in, your Google name and email address.
  • Verification details: information from your ID, which you provide before receiving a reward, to confirm that you are who you say you are and are eligible to win.
  • Payment and payout details: records of entry fees, hint purchases, Vault top-ups and withdrawals, and the bank name, account name and account number you give us for payouts. Card payments are handled by Paystack or Flutterwave. We never receive your full card details.
  • Hunt activity: hunts you join, answers you submit, the time of each submission, stages completed, hints used, results, rewards and referral activity.
  • Device and usage data: IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, actions in the app, error logs and approximate location derived from your IP address. We use these to keep hunts fair and the Platform working.
  • Messages: emails, support requests, feedback, surveys and anything you send us on WhatsApp or social media.
  • Content you share: public posts in which you tag or mention SPOILS, and any stories, photos or videos you choose to send us.

We do not need, and ask you not to send, sensitive personal data such as health information, religious beliefs or biometric data.

03Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To run your account and hunts, verify winners and pay rewards, because this is necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • To prevent cheating and fraud, secure the Platform and improve SPOILS, because we have a legitimate interest in fair, safe and working hunts. We balance this against your rights.
  • To keep financial and tax records, check eligibility and respond to lawful requests from authorities, because the law requires us to.
  • To send marketing emails and messages, use analytics cookies on our website and feature your name, photo or story, only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Service messages, such as sign-in codes, password resets, hunt reminders and reward updates, are part of the service. Marketing messages, such as news about upcoming hunts, are optional. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.

04What other hunters and the public can see

  • Leaderboards, live activity feeds, the Hall of Hunters and public hunter profiles show your hunter name, profile picture (if you add one), positions and hunt statistics. They never show your legal name, email address or phone number.
  • We keep winners' legal names off our website. We only publish your name, photo or story if you agree.
  • In our WhatsApp community, your phone number and profile may be visible to other members, depending on WhatsApp's own settings. WhatsApp's privacy policy applies to your use of WhatsApp.

05Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data and we never share it for third-party advertising. We share it only as needed with:

  • Service providers who work for us: Supabase (database and storage), Render and Cloudflare (hosting, security and delivery), Resend (service emails), Sender (newsletters), Paystack and Flutterwave (payments and payouts), Google (sign-in and website analytics) and Meta (WhatsApp community). They may only use your data to provide their service to us.
  • Reward partners, when a partner delivers a reward to you, such as an event ticket. We share only what's needed to deliver it, and tell you beforehand.
  • Authorities and advisers, where the law requires it, to protect hunters or the public, or to establish or defend legal claims.
  • A future owner of SPOILS, if the business is sold or merged. Your data stays protected by this policy, and we'll tell you before it moves.

Partners and sponsors may receive reports about a hunt, such as how many hunters took part. These reports don't identify you.

06Data stored outside Nigeria

Some of our service providers store or process data outside Nigeria, including in the United States and the European Union. When your data leaves Nigeria, we make sure the transfer is lawful under the NDPA and the GAID. We rely on the safeguards these laws recognise, such as the provider's contractual data protection commitments and the protection level of the destination country.

07How long we keep it

  • Account details: while your account is open. They're deleted or anonymised within 30 days of closure, unless we need them for an open dispute or a legal requirement.
  • Hunt activity: 24 months after the hunt, then deleted or anonymised. We keep anonymised results and hunter names on winners' records and leaderboards.
  • Verification records: as long as your account is open, then 12 months.
  • Payment, reward and payout records: 7 years from the transaction, to meet Nigerian tax and financial record-keeping rules.
  • Device and usage logs: 12 months.
  • Support messages: 24 months.
  • Marketing consent records: while you're subscribed, plus 3 years.

08How we protect it

All data travels over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections. Bank details are encrypted when stored. Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text. Database access is restricted by row-level security. Sign-in attempts are rate-limited, and accounts are locked after repeated failed attempts. Only team members who need your data to do their job can access it.

If a personal data breach puts your rights at risk, we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, as the NDPA requires. If the risk to you is high, we will also tell you promptly, in plain language, with steps you can take.

09Automated checks

The Platform automatically records answers and finish times to rank hunters, and flags unusual activity that may be cheating. A member of our team reviews any flag before a disqualification or the withholding of a reward. You can ask us to explain a decision and to have it reconsidered.

10Your rights

Under the NDPA, you have the right to:

  • be told how your data is used, which is what this policy does;
  • get a copy of your data;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • have your data deleted, unless we must keep it by law;
  • restrict or object to how we use it, including for marketing at any time;
  • receive your data in a common, machine-readable format, and have it sent to another organisation where that's technically possible;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting anything done before;
  • avoid decisions made only by automated means that significantly affect you;
  • complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at ndpc.gov.ng.

To make a request, email hello@spoils.live with "Privacy request" in the subject line. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We'll acknowledge your request within 7 days and respond fully within 30 days. This is free of charge, unless a request is clearly excessive.

If you are visiting our website from outside Nigeria, you can exercise these rights too. You also keep any extra rights the privacy laws of your country give you.

11Under-18s

SPOILS is only for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we'll close the account and delete the data, keeping only what the law requires.

12Links to other sites

Hunts often send you to other websites and apps. Their own privacy policies apply there, and we aren't responsible for how they handle your data.

13Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we'll email registered hunters and post a notice on the Platform at least 14 days before it takes effect.

Cookies

Version 2.0 · Published 24 October 2026 · Part of our Privacy Policy.

Cookies and similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, are small files that let a website remember things. We use two kinds:

  • Essential: needed for the site and app to work, for example keeping you signed in, protecting forms from abuse, and remembering your cookie choice. These are always on.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics, which tells us which pages people visit and how they find us, so we can improve the site. These only run if you choose "Accept all" on our cookie notice.

We don't use advertising cookies, and we don't track you across other websites. To change your choice, clear this site's cookies and storage in your browser. The notice will appear again on your next visit, and you can choose again.
