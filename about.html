About

SPOILS makes curiosity competitive.

We believe curiosity deserves more than an answer. It deserves an experience.

Why we exist

People have more entertainment than ever, and most of it asks them to watch, scroll or react. Very little asks them to investigate, connect information and make a discovery for themselves.

SPOILS exists for the people who can't leave a mystery unfinished. We turn clues, stories and familiar online spaces into live hunts, so the pleasure of figuring something out becomes something you can play, compete in and win.

What we learned early

The chase is the thrill.

We ran our first hunt in March 2026. One of our strongest hunts since then had no prize at all, and hunters still competed, posted and argued about it in public. That told us what SPOILS is really about. The chase is the thrill, and the reward completes it.

What we stand for.

  1. The experience comes first.

    A hunt should be worth playing even if you don't win.

  2. Mystery, never confusion.

    The story can be hard. The rules, entry and eligibility are always clear.

  3. Competition without humiliation.

    We celebrate great performances without making anyone feel small for missing a clue.

  4. Fair play, every time.

    Clear rules, a transparent way of deciding winners, and rewards delivered as promised.

  5. Winners own their privacy.

    You can claim the SPOILS without your name ending up on our website.

Where we're going.

We're starting with digital hunts in Nigeria. Over time, we want every screen, street, story and brand to be the possible start of a hunt.
