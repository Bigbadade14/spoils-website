About SPOILS

What is SPOILS?
SPOILS is a competitive entertainment platform based in Nigeria. We run digital treasure hunts where participants, called hunters (yes, that's you), find the answers to clues hidden across real websites, social media pages and the internet, solve each stage, and race other hunters to the finish. The fastest hunters to solve every stage claim the SPOILS: a real reward such as cash, event tickets, food vouchers or gadgets. Prizes can be anything.
Is SPOILS gambling or betting?
No. SPOILS is a competition of observation, reasoning and speed. Nobody wins by chance or by predicting outcomes. Rewards go to the hunters whose correct final answers are recorded within the hunt's winning places, which are stated before you join.
Is SPOILS legit?
Yes. SPOILS has run public hunts since March 2026, and hunters have claimed more than ₦100,000 worth of rewards. Every hunt and its reward is on our Winners page, and winners who choose to share their story appear on our Instagram, @spoilsapp.
Who runs SPOILS?
SPOILS is a product of SPOILS Technology Limited.

Playing a hunt

How does a SPOILS hunt work?
Join the hunt before it opens. At start time, every hunter gets the first clue at once. Each answer points to the next clue somewhere on the internet. Enter your answers on SPOILS as you go. The hunters who finish within the hunt's winning places claim the SPOILS. Every hunt states its number of winners in advance, with the reward for each place, from first to last.
Do I need to be a genius to play?
No. SPOILS rewards curiosity, attention and persistence. If you enjoy noticing details and working things out, you're a hunter. You'll get sharper the more you play.
What happens if I don't win?
You still get the chase, and there's always another hunt. Join the Hunters Club to hear when the next one surfaces.
How do I sign up?
Sign up at www.spoils.live and create your hunter account.
Who can play?
Anyone in Nigeria aged 18 or over.
Do I need to be in Lagos?
No. Every hunt is played online, so you can join from anywhere in Nigeria with a phone or laptop and an internet connection.
Can I play from outside Nigeria?
Not yet. SPOILS is open to hunters in Nigeria for now, and we plan to open up to more countries. Join the Hunters Club to hear first.
How long does a hunt last?
It depends on the trail. Short hunts with a few stages can be over in minutes. Harder hunts with more stages can take hours.
Are there hints?
Some hunts have hints, others don't, and in some hunts you have to buy hints when you need them.
Can I play with friends?
You can play against them. Every hunt is solo, so each hunter plays on their own account and solves alone.
How often are hunts?
We run new hunts regularly. Join the Hunters Club on WhatsApp or follow @spoilsapp on Instagram to hear about the next one first.

Rewards and entry

What can I win?
Every hunt names its reward before you join. Hunters have claimed cash, event tickets and food vouchers, and future rewards could be something else entirely.
How do winners receive their reward?
Every winner goes through a quick verification first. Cash is then sent by bank transfer or credited to your SPOILS wallet. Non-cash rewards, like event tickets and food vouchers, reach you within 5 to 7 working days.
Does it cost money to join?
Some hunts are free and most have an entry fee. The fee is always shown on the hunt page before you join.

Fair play and privacy

How are winners decided?
Correct final answers and finish times are recorded by the platform. Every hunt states its number of winners in advance, with the reward for each place, from first to last. We review every winning run and verify each winner before any reward goes out.
What stops people from cheating?
Each person gets one account, finish times are recorded by the platform, and sharing answers during a live hunt gets you disqualified.
Can SPOILS team members play?
SPOILS staff and anyone who helped build a hunt can't compete for its reward.
Will my name be shown if I win?
No. We keep winners' names off our website. If you'd like to share your story, we'll create content with you, but only with your permission.
Is my data safe?
We only collect what we need to run hunts, verify winners and deliver rewards. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
